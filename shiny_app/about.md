## About

This app contains scATAC-seq data from first trimester human fetal liver, primarily in the form of transcription factor motif activity calculated using ChromVAR.

The dataset is presented in a [preprint at biorXiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2025.12.02.691283v1).
